FAQ

Questions? Answered.

Everything you might want to know before getting started. Still curious? Email us at hello@lumina.io.

How long does it take to set up Lumina?

Most teams are live within 30 minutes. One-click connectors handle the heavy lifting for popular sources like Stripe, Salesforce and Postgres, and our templates give you a polished dashboard from day one.

Do I need a credit card for the free plan?

No. The Starter plan is free forever with no card required. You only enter payment details if you choose to upgrade to Pro or Enterprise.

Can I migrate my data from other analytics tools?

Yes. Lumina imports historical data from Mixpanel, Amplitude, Google Analytics and CSV exports, and our team runs the migration with you on Pro and Enterprise plans.

Is my data secure and compliant?

Lumina is SOC 2 Type II certified and GDPR compliant. All data is encrypted in transit and at rest, with SSO/SAML, role-based access and full audit logs on Enterprise.

What happens if I exceed my event limit?

Nothing breaks. We keep collecting your data and notify you about the overage — you can upgrade, buy extra events, or we will simply keep going and settle it at the end of the cycle. We never drop your data.

Can I cancel my subscription anytime?

Yes — cancellation takes two clicks in settings, effective at the end of your billing period. Your dashboards become read-only rather than disappearing, so you never lose access to your history.
